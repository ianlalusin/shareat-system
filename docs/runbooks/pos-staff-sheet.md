# New POS: one-page sheet for cashiers, servers and the kitchen

**Purpose:** a short, plain sheet staff can keep at the till. **Owner:** Lipa manager (prints and explains it); Ian or the planner adds the Tagalog lines. Written for T-533 (R4), 15 Nov 2026.

> Lines that start with **Tagalog:** are placeholders. Someone fills them in before this sheet is printed. English stays as written.

The POS is at **erp2.shareat.net/pos**. The tabs are: **Orders, Kitchen, Refills, Receipts, Till**.

## 1. Sign in
- Tap **Employee ID**, then **PIN**, on the on-screen keypad. Nothing is typed on the keyboard.
- Wrong PIN: try again slowly. If it keeps failing, call the manager.
- Changing cashier: tap **Switch cashier**, then the new person signs in with their own Employee ID + PIN. Open tables stay.
- Tagalog: _(how to sign in; switching cashier)_

## 2. Seat a table
1. **Orders** tab, then **Seat a party**.
2. Pick the **stand** (table number). Pick the **package**. Enter the **heads** (how many people).
3. Tap dishes to add them. Check **Items so far**.
4. Tap **Send to kitchen**.
- Take-out: **Take-out**, then add dishes, then **Send take-out order**.
- Tagalog: _(seat a table; heads; send to kitchen)_

## 3. Refills and heads
- **Refills** tab: pick the table (open package tables only), tap **+** on each meat, then send.
- Change the number of people: **Heads**, then **Confirm**.
- Tagalog: _(refills; changing heads)_

## 4. Kitchen
- **Kitchen** tab: you see each table's dishes that are not served yet. Oldest first.
- When a dish is served, tap **Served**. **All out** marks every dish on that table served.
- If you see only printed tickets, the internet is down. See section 7.
- Tagalog: _(kitchen: Served / All out)_

## 5. Pay
1. On the table, tap **Pay**. Check the bill.
2. Tap **Discount** for a senior citizen or PWD. Enter the name on the card and the ID number. **One discount card per person.** Check the ID is real and the same person.
3. Tap a way to pay (**Cash**, **GCash**, ...). Enter the amount. For **GCash**, enter the **reference number**.
4. Split payment: add a second way to pay for the rest.
5. Finish. The sale shows under **Receipts**.
- To remove a discount or cancel a line after sending, ask the **manager**. They enter their own Employee ID + PIN.
- Tagalog: _(pay; senior/PWD; GCash reference; split)_

## 6. End of day (manager only)
- **Till** tab. First read **Reading so far** (the X reading). It does not close the day.
- Then **Close day**. The manager enters their own Employee ID + PIN. The Z-reading closes.
- Needs the **internet**. If it says "Closing the day needs the internet.", wait, then try again.
- Cash in the drawer is counted in the ERP till screen ("Till counts and handover"), not on the POS.
- Tagalog: _(close the day)_

## 7. If the internet goes down
- A **yellow band** shows at the top. You can still **seat, add dishes and take Cash**.
- You **cannot** use card or QR Ph, refund, or close the day. Do not close or reload the page.
- GCash or Maya offline: only with the reference number. The manager checks it later.
- Printed kitchen tickets may come from the tablet while offline. Take them to the kitchen.
- When the internet is back, the tablet sends the sales by itself. **Wait until Unsent sales says 0** (Settings > Unsent sales) before you turn the tablet off or sign out for the night.
- Tagalog: _(no internet: what still works; wait for 0 unsent)_

## 8. Never do
- Never clear the browser's data or cache, never close the tab, and never sign the tablet out while sales are unsent.
- Never share a PIN. Never use someone else's Employee ID.
- Never type a customer's ID number anywhere except the discount box.
- Tagalog: _(the three nevers)_
