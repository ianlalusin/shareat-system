What this option means

For: it finishes the half-built dark mode and fills the missing dark brand, warning and success values. The big targets suit clock terminals, counts and receiving, and it's dim enough for a restaurant floor at night.

Against: as the only theme it breaks ADR-013's light default, and accountants reading statements prefer light. 52px controls cost density on report pages.

Recommended use: the dark-toggle values plus a "floor" density for /clock, /till, counts and deliveries, layered on A or B.
