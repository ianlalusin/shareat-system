What this option means

For: one brand across the Website, menu and ERP. Baloo 2 and pills are what v1 staff already know, and cream is easier on the eyes than white during long count sessions. ADR-013's rules (one red, outlined danger, amber only as a surface) still hold.

Against: cream lowers contrast on dense tables slightly; it's still 14:1 for ink, but the zebra and border steps need care. Pills take more width in tight toolbars.

Cost: Option A's work, plus a second font, cream and gold tokens, and a rule on ADR-013 amendment 3 (radius).
