What mattersPillRounded rectangle
Telling a button from a badge. Status badges and filter chips are pills in every option. If buttons are pills too, "Approve" and "Submitted" in the same row have the same shape, and only colour says which one you can press✗ same shape✓ clearly different
Matching the fields. Inputs, selects and cards are rounded rectangles. v1 put pill buttons next to rectangular inputs, and the audit flagged it as a mismatch✗ repeats v1's mismatch✓ one family
Density. A pill needs about 6px more padding on each side to look balanced, so a 4-button toolbar is about 48px wider. That matters on tables and toolbars, the ERP's main surfacewider✓ tighter
Groups and icon buttons. Segmented controls, split buttons and the 44px icon button join and align more cleanly as rectangles. A pill icon button is a circleworks, looks softer✓ cleaner
Brand match. The Website's buttons are pills, and v1's staff are used to pills✓ matchesThe brand still shows through Baloo 2, cream, the red and the logo
Warmth and approachability. A pill reads friendlier, which suits the clock terminal and the staff app✓ friendlier12px corners are soft, not sharp
My pick

Rounded rectangle, 12px, for every button and field across the app. Pills are kept for things you read or filter by: status badges, filter chips and counts. Buttons and badges then never look alike, and buttons, fields and cards share one shape family. The brand still comes from type, colour and the logo. It wins 4 of the 6 points; the pill wins on brand match and warmth. This reverses the "pill buttons" line in ADR-013 Amendment 7, so the decision is yours.
