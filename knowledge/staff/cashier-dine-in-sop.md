# Cashier SOP — dine-in

A cashier must be on duty at all times.

## Taking the order

1. Hand the customer the menu and explain it.
2. Ask which package and which flavors they want.
3. Enter the order in the POS.
4. Ask for the customer's name, how many are in the group, and any notes, such as allergies.
   Enter them — the kitchen sees everything you enter, notes included.
5. Start the session on the POS. The POS prints a QR code.
6. Explain the QR code: scanning it opens the customer app, where they can order on their own.
7. Explain the house rules: the [no-leftover policy](../policies/leftover-policy.md) and how to
   order more.
8. Seat the customer (or have a server do it) and give them their table number.

## Billing

9. When the customer comes to settle, have a server inspect the table for leftovers and any
   extra orders.
10. Confirm any extra orders with the customer and explain any leftover charge.
11. Give the bill at the counter. The POS prints a **bill only, never an invoice** — the POS is
    not BIR-accredited (v1 and v2).
12. Take payment using any method the store accepts.
13. If the customer asks for an official receipt, write one by hand on the manual OR booklet at
    the counter and give it to them.
14. Settling the payment ends the session automatically.

## Allergies

Enter every allergy or dietary note — the kitchen sees it. A no-pork table gets no pork all
session and a clean setup. Buffet side dishes cannot be adjusted for allergies; à la carte can.
Never call anything "halal". Full rules:
[allergies and dietary requests](../policies/allergies-and-dietary-requests.md).
