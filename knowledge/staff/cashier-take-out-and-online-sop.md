# Cashier SOP — take-out and online orders

## Walk-in take-out

1. Open the à la carte tab on the POS.
2. Get the customer's name, and their contact number if they give it.
3. Start the session, then add the à la carte items. The order goes to the kitchen tablet, the
   same way as a dine-in session.
4. Payment:
   - A customer waiting in the waiting area may pay now or when they get the food.
   - A customer who is leaving and coming back must pay **before leaving**.
5. The kitchen brings the finished order to you. Hand it to the customer.

## Website orders (shareat.net)

- New website orders appear in the POS **online orders** tab. The tab shows a number badge with
  how many orders are waiting — check it so no order is missed.
- Which items appear online, and their online prices, are set by a manager or admin on the POS
  admin menu page.

## Facebook, call, and text orders

- These come straight to the store. Take the order and send it to the kitchen.

## Delivery (website, Facebook, call, text)

1. Book a rider through a delivery app: PandaGo, Grab Padala, Lalamove, or another service.
2. **Payment must be received before the rider is dispatched.**
   - Cash on delivery is possible only with PandaGo, and only when PandaGo is available.
   - Otherwise, collect payment online first: GCash, bank transfer, or another e-wallet.
3. Encourage the customer to pay right after ordering. In practice the kitchen usually starts
   cooking right away while you sort out the payment with the customer.

## Pickup (website, Facebook, call, text)

- Encourage online payment after ordering. If the customer asks, they may pay at the counter on
  pickup.

## Foodpanda and GrabFood

- Foodpanda orders come in on the store's Foodpanda tablet. Grab orders come in on the Grab
  Merchant app on the company phone.
- Whoever holds the device handles the order. If you hold it, pass each order to the kitchen.
- The customer pays Foodpanda or Grab, never the store. These orders are not entered in the POS.
