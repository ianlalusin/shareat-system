# Future franchised stores — manager training and release

This page describes future franchised stores. Company-owned stores follow their own pages.

## The two training files

1. Manager Guide: plain steps, tick-lists and a glossary. It is for the manager.
2. Trainer Notes: answer keys, which ERP features are live and which are coming, how to run each check, and the monthly sign-off sheet. It is for the trainer.

Trainer Notes also hold one worked example per module: a sample situation with made-up numbers and the model answer. Real store records may replace them later.

## How training works

- The trainer shows each task, the manager practises it, then the trainer watches it done in the store.
- Each module ends with a tick-list. Items marked must-pass must be done correctly before release.
- There is no fixed schedule. The manager moves forward by showing competence, judged at the monthly checks.
- Practice uses sample records. Never create false sales, charges or stock records in the live store.
- Features that are (coming) use a paper record. Paper records count the same as ERP records.

## Modules added

Food safety and sanitation. Allergens and the no-pork setup (never claim halal). Recipe portions and brand standards. Complaint handling. Data privacy.

Food safety and the no-pork setup are must-pass. Must-pass items also include cash closing, an emergency drill and ERP records.

## Hard situations

The trainer talks through six situations with the manager. There is no role-play.

1. Freezer failure at night with the owner and deputy unreachable.
2. Staff no-show.
3. Angry customer.
4. Core-meat supplier failure.
5. Cash-shortage conversation.
6. POS outage at dinner.

## Release

- The trainer checks progress once a month.
- Release needs every must-pass item ticked on two separate monthly checks.
- The trainer, SharEat and the franchisee all sign both checks.
- Release also needs the two agreements on the provided manager page: SharEat's readiness checklist, and the franchisee's ready successor.

## Keeping the owner informed

- Weekly 5-line brief from the manager to the franchisee: cash short or over, stock differences, open issues, spending against budget, items awaiting approval.
- Owner monthly spot-check: refunds over the limit, stock changes, cash paid out, remittance slips and staff charges.

## Training a replacement

- The franchisee or the outgoing manager may train the new manager with the same tick-lists.
- The Trainer Notes name which checks need no ERP.
- SharEat retraining stays available at the Annex B rate.
- The franchisee signs off alone for their own hire.
