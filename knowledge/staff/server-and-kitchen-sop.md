# Server and kitchen SOP

## Servers

1. If you are the first to see an arriving customer, greet them and bring them to the cashier.
2. Seat the customer at their table and give them their table number, if the cashier has not.
3. Customers may ask you for refills. Order them on your device.
4. When the kitchen rings the bell (or calls out), take the ready order to the table.
5. When your phone shows an "ask for assistance" alert, go to that table.
6. At billing, when the cashier asks, inspect the table for leftovers and any extra orders, and
   report back.

## Kitchen

1. The kitchen device shows the first order the cashier entered — package, meat, flavors, and
   notes, including allergies — and every refill ordered afterwards from the customer app or a
   server's device.
2. Prepare each order. Refills are mostly meat, rice, and cheese; side dishes and drinks are
   self-service at the buffet table.
3. When an order is ready, ring the bell for a server. If there is no bell, call a server out.
4. The kitchen tablet also shows "ask for assistance" alerts.
5. No-pork tables: no pork for the whole session, and start them with clean tongs, clean plates,
   and clean everything. Cook à la carte orders with an allergy note separately, with separate
   tongs. Buffet side dishes are batch-cooked and cannot be adjusted. Full rules:
   [allergies and dietary requests](../policies/allergies-and-dietary-requests.md).
6. Take-out orders arrive on the kitchen tablet the same way as dine-in. Prepare them and bring
   them to the cashier.
7. If the Foodpanda tablet or the Grab Merchant phone is kept in the kitchen, handle those orders
   directly from the device.
