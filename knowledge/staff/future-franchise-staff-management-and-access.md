# Future franchise staff management payroll and access

Future-franchise policy approved on 4 November 2026. Existing contracts remain separate.

## Schedules leave and overtime

- The manager prepares weekly staff shifts and rest days within franchisee-approved staffing
  and working-hour limits. The manager's own schedule requires franchisee approval.
- The manager approves staff leave while maintaining coverage. The franchisee approves manager leave.
- **When the franchisee is absent**, the manager may decide staff overtime and **their own
  overtime**, always recording the authorization and reason in ERP.
- Overtime limits, notification timing and the procedure when the franchisee is present were
  not set. Do not erase the expressly approved manager-own-overtime exception.

## Payroll and employment administration

- **Payroll is solely the franchisee's work:** preparation, verification and processing.
- The franchisee checks records and confirms doubtful or missing entries with the manager.
- The manager supplies clarification when consulted; no mandatory manager pre-payroll check was adopted.
- The franchisee handles permits/renewals, tax filings and required employee contributions.
  The manager supplies complete operational records and flags deadlines.
- The manager coaches staff, documents warnings/incidents and logs infractions promptly in ERP.
- The franchisee decides suspension or termination after review; employment procedures remain separate.

## ERP and POS accounts

1. The franchisee approves requests for new staff accounts or access changes.
2. SharEat administers the permissions as ERP/POS administrator.
3. When someone leaves, the franchisee **immediately informs SharEat to request removal**.
4. **Only SharEat removes ERP/POS access.** No SharEat response-time guarantee was specified.

No actual accounts or software permissions were changed by this policy discussion.

## Manager transition

- The outgoing manager hands over cash, stock, keys, records and pending matters to the incoming manager.
- If no incoming manager is in place, the franchisee receives the handover and takes over management.
- The provided manager returns to SharEat after the assignment and handover, within the support cap.
- A separate franchisee supervision/sign-off procedure was not confirmed merely by naming the recipient.

## Social-media access

The franchisee manages the store's social accounts and may give the manager and cashier access
limited to posting, messaging and commenting. The franchisee immediately removes that access
when either leaves. This differs from ERP/POS removal by SharEat. See
[brand and bookings](../company/future-franchise-brand-and-bookings.md).
