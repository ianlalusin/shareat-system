# Future franchise hiring training and provided manager

Future-franchise policy approved on 4 November 2026. Existing contracts remain separate.

## Initial staff service

1. SharEat screens, makes the final candidate selection and trains **up to seven staff,
   depending on store needs**. Positions: cashiers, servers, kitchen staff and dishwashers.
2. The franchisee and each new hire agree salary/rates and benefits. SharEat only screens,
   selects and trains; it does not commit the franchisee to compensation terms.
3. The franchisee handles employment setup, applicable enrollment, medical requirements and
   related paperwork. Uniforms are excluded from the future package.
4. The provided manager is additional to this staff allocation. No per-position headcount is fixed.

## Provided manager

- SharEat provides a manager for an initial **one month from opening**. Deployment may continue
  according to SharEat's readiness evaluation, with an **absolute maximum of six months**.
- SharEat evaluates whether the franchisee can operate personally or their own manager is ready.
- The franchisee is the intended employer during deployment and pays the manager's salary,
  meals during work, accommodation when needed and travel allowance when needed.
- Meals outside work are the manager's own expense. SharEat provides the associated training free.
- After deployment and handover, the provided manager returns to SharEat.
- The six-month support cap is not automatic employment termination or permission for permanent
  franchisee retention. Employment/return documentation must match the agreed arrangement.
- No specific salary, allowance rate, travel eligibility or accommodation standard was set.

## Replacement and additional hires

1. The manager screens and recommends candidates using SharEat hiring guidelines.
2. The franchisee gives final hiring approval, agrees pay/benefits with the employee and handles
   employment setup. This later-hire approval differs from SharEat's initial candidate selection.
3. The manager coaches and trains new hires using SharEat guidelines and the ERP onboarding feature.
4. The manager verifies onboarding/training completion and readiness before independent work.
5. For new-hire probation reviews, the manager assesses performance and recommends the outcome;
   the franchisee makes the final employment decision. No probation length was decided here.

ERP onboarding support is described by Ian; this is not a separate deployment verification.
SharEat evaluates the provided manager's handover readiness. Detailed competency/sign-off forms
remain for the relevant procedures. Extra SharEat-led retraining follows the quote/approval rule
in [support and reviews](../company/future-franchise-support-and-reviews.md).
