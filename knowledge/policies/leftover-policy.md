# No-leftover policy

- Leftover food is charged, and leftovers may never be taken home.
- The cashier explains this rule before the customer is seated.
- At billing, a server checks the table for leftovers.
- There are fixed leftover charges for meat, dip sauce, and side dishes. The amounts are set in
  the POS and can change; always quote the current amount from the system.
- Usually one charge is made per table. The server decides, based on how much is left, whether
  to charge more (for example, per plate) or less.
- The cashier explains any leftover charge to the customer before giving the bill.
