# Allergies and dietary requests

Customers tell the cashier about allergies or dietary needs when ordering. The cashier enters
them as notes, and the kitchen sees them.

**Our kitchen is shared.** The same grills, tongs, plates, fryers, prep areas and storage are
used for many dishes. We take care with every request, but we cannot promise that any dish or
table is completely free of any ingredient or allergen.

If you have a serious allergy, please tell the cashier before you order and ask to talk to the
manager on duty. The manager will tell you honestly what we can and cannot do for you. If we
cannot serve you safely, we will tell you.

Shareat is not halal-certified, so we never describe Shareat or any table as "halal".

## No pork

- A table that does not eat pork (for example, Muslim customers) is served **no pork for the
  whole session**.
- That table starts with clean tongs, clean plates and a clean setup. Pork is still cooked in the
  same kitchen, so we cannot promise there is no trace of pork.

## Allergens

- **Nuts:** we do not add nuts to our dishes. Some sauces and supplied ingredients may contain
  nuts or be made where nuts are handled, so we cannot promise a dish is nut-free.
- **Shrimp and seafood:** served only when a customer orders them. They are cooked in the same
  kitchen as other food, so we cannot promise a dish has no seafood.
- **Soy:** used in some dishes and sauces. We cannot promise a dish has no soy.

## Leaving items out

- Requests such as "no kimchi" or "no pickle" are followed.

## What we can and cannot control

- **Unlimited side dishes (buffet):** cooked in batches, so we cannot adjust them for a single
  customer's allergy.
- **À la carte items:** cooked one order at a time, with separate tongs, so we can take more care
  with allergy requests. They are still made in the shared kitchen.
