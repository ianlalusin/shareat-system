# Future franchised stores — safety and incidents

This page describes future franchised stores. Company-owned stores follow their own pages.

- Report urgent matters, such as lost items, to the franchisee at once.
- A serious incident is food safety, injury, or anything that could hurt SharEat's name. The manager tells the franchisee and SharEat at once, and within 1 hour at the latest.
- For a serious, immediate safety risk, the manager may stop the affected service or close the store without waiting for approval.

## Reopening

- After a closure for food safety, injury or a government order: the franchisee sends SharEat the cause and the fix. The store reopens once SharEat agrees.
- After other closures (for example a power cut or flood): the franchisee decides when to reopen.

## Public replies

- The franchisee decides public replies to serious complaints, within SharEat brand standards, and tells SharEat within 1 hour.
- Staff posting access does not include this decision.
