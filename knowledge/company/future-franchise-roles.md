# Future franchised stores — roles, powers and data

This page describes future franchised stores. Company-owned stores follow their own pages.

If this page and the franchise agreement disagree, the franchise agreement wins.

## The roles

| Role | Who | Main job |
|---|---|---|
| SharEat | Head office and franchisor | Trains the first team, provides a manager at the start, supports the store, adds new products to the menu, runs the ERP and POS (gives and removes access), does the yearly store review |
| Franchisee | The store's owner | Owns and funds the business. Employs all staff. Handles payroll, permits, taxes, settlement and large events |
| Named deputy | One person the franchisee names in writing | Acts on the franchisee's money duties while the franchisee is away, within the limits on Annex A. The deputy is not the store manager |
| Store manager | The SharEat-provided manager at the start, then the franchisee's own manager (or the franchisee personally) | Runs daily operations, supervises staff, checks records, reports problems |
| Staff | Cashiers, servers, kitchen staff, dishwashers, maintenance and others | Do the tasks assigned to them |

In the system, the franchisee is the store's administrator and its manager by default. When the store has a manager, the franchisee hands the manager role to that person. The franchisee keeps the administrator role.

## Two rules to remember

- Only the powers written down exist. Doing a task never gives extra authority.
- Approving something is separate from paying for it.

## When the franchisee is away

The franchisee names one deputy in writing on Annex C, such as a spouse, business partner or bookkeeper.

While the franchisee is away or cannot be reached, the deputy may do the franchisee's money tasks within the limits on Annex A:

1. Confirm remittances.
2. Approve stock corrections.
3. Pay suppliers.
4. Approve refunds or repairs above the manager's limits.

The manager's own powers do not change. The person who handles the cash is never the one who confirms it arrived.

A named deputy in the ERP is (coming). Until then, keep the written naming on paper.

## The three annexes

| Annex | What it holds | When |
|---|---|---|
| Annex A: Store limits | Every peso limit and the overtime hours, filled in by the franchisee | Signed by both SharEat and the franchisee before the store opens. A limit changes later only with both signatures |
| Annex B: What you pay SharEat | Every charge beyond the franchise fee. A charge not listed is not payable | Filled in and signed before opening |
| Annex C: Store details | Deputy, opening/closing backup, remittance schedule, count lists, report cutoff date, urgent contacts, provided-manager dates, yearly review month | Agreed before opening |

Peso limits are never written on these pages. Always look at the store's own Annex A.

## Features marked "(coming)"

Some ERP and POS features are not live yet. Until they are, the store keeps a paper or spreadsheet record of the same thing. The list is on the planned page.

## Data

- The franchisee owns the store's data and may export it.
- SharEat sees the store's sales and stock levels only.
- SharEat sees anything more (for example expenses, payroll or staff charges) only with the franchisee's consent.
- Every such access is logged.
