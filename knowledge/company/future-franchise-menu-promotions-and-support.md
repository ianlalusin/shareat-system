# Future franchised stores — menu, promotions, support and yearly review

This page describes future franchised stores. Company-owned stores follow their own pages.

Menu items and prices are never written on a page. See the current menu in the system.

## Menu, prices and promotions

- Prices: the store manager may set the store's menu prices in the system.
- New products: the franchisee or manager requests them through the ERP. SharEat answers within 7 days and adds approved items to the menu.
- Local promotions: the franchisee may run its own store promotions.
- SharEat promotions: all are voluntary. SharEat announces each one. The franchisee chooses whether to join.
- Cashiers cannot create offers or change prices.

## Social media

- The franchisee runs the store's accounts within SharEat brand standards.
- The manager and cashier may be given posting, messaging and commenting access. The franchisee removes it the day they leave.
- SharEat may moderate when a post breaks brand standards.
- Staff posting access does not include public replies to serious complaints. The franchisee decides those.

## SharEat support

- Visits: SharEat visits every 2 weeks for the first 3 months. In months 4 to 12 it checks in monthly (on site or online). After the first year, visits are arranged when needed. Visits continue after the provided manager leaves.
- Support: SharEat gives online support during operating hours, and advisory or on-site help as set in the franchise agreement. Costs are on Annex B.
- Response time: SharEat answers every request within 7 days. The one exception is access removal, which takes up to 72 hours.
- Retraining: extra SharEat retraining beyond the included training is quoted first and scheduled only with the franchisee's approval. Rates are on Annex B. Included training stays free.

## Yearly review and standards

- Yearly store review: SharEat evaluates the store's food and service quality, brand standards and training, with the franchisee.
- After the review, the manager makes the operational fixes. The franchisee approves the spending and makes sure the fixes are done. SharEat checks them.
- When standards are not met: SharEat gives a written warning and re-inspects. No other penalty is set.
- Manager review: each year the franchisee reviews the manager using ERP results, staff development and SharEat standards.
