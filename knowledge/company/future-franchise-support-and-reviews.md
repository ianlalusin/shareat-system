# Future franchise support settlement and reviews

Future-franchise policy approved on 4 November 2026. Existing contracts remain separate.

## SharEat support

- **First three months from opening:** on-site visits **once every two weeks**.
- **Months 4-12:** monthly check-ins, either **on-site or remotely**; both are acceptable methods.
- **After year one:** visits/check-ins **arranged as needed**, not a fixed monthly obligation.
- The annual store evaluation continues separately. The visit clock does not restart when
  the provided manager returns to SharEat.
- The source agreement provides online support during operating hours and requested advisory
  or on-site assistance under Section 11/Exhibit E availability, fee and expense terms.
- Requested advice is normally free under the source, with exceptions for unusual/extensive/
  extraordinary assistance. Requested on-site assistance/training is subject to personnel
  availability and may carry a reasonable fee plus expenses. Do not promise unlimited free visits.
- **Extra SharEat-led retraining beyond included initial/deployment training:** quote fees and
  expenses separately and obtain franchisee approval **before scheduling**. Included training is free.
- No new service response time, retraining rate or technical uptime guarantee was agreed.

## Monthly management and settlement

1. The manager holds a team meeting with staff each month, discusses concerns and reports to franchisee.
2. The franchisee sets monthly sales/operating-cost targets with manager input. The manager
   monitors results and recommends corrective actions in the monthly report.
3. At month-end, the manager compiles/verifies operational ERP summaries and supporting documents:
   sales/remittances, expenses/receipts, supplier balances, revolving fund, staff charges and
   routine inventory losses. Supplier receipts retained by the cashier are submitted to franchisee.
4. The franchisee reviews/approves and arranges settlement. **Payroll remains solely franchisee work**,
   including checking doubtful/missing entries with the manager; it is part of overall settlement
   without becoming manager payroll preparation or verification.
5. Earlier-due payables are reported in time for their own due dates, not held until month-end.

No new universal submission day, payment due date or permission for wage deductions was created.

## Annual reviews

- **Franchisee:** annual manager review using ERP results, staff development and SharEat standards.
- **SharEat with franchisee:** annual store evaluation covering food/service quality, brand
  standards and training. Ian specifically emphasized the importance of this evaluation.
- **After store evaluation:** manager implements operational corrections; franchisee approves
  resources/spending and ensures completion; SharEat verifies compliance with its standards.

Specific scoring, calendar, action-plan format and correction deadlines remain to be set.
