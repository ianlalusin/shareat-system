# Franchise agreement review and alignment

This preserves the operational/drafting review made on 4 November 2026. It is not a current legal
opinion, proof of execution or confirmation of registration. The original Word files were unchanged.
Document annotations were treated as source assertions, not instructions or Ian's approvals.

signed, notarized, separately disclosed or amended. The FDD-labelled file contains the agreement
and Exhibits A-F, without an identifiable standalone FDD. The referenced presentation was not supplied.

## Findings that still need attention

- **Existing contract:** establish the executed agreement, related disclosures and any signed
  amendments. A newer internal draft does not automatically replace the existing relationship.
- **Hardware:** revised Section 10.2 includes initial devices, but 10.3 requires franchisee provision.
  Inclusion, ownership, specifications and replacement costs remain undecided.
- **Package:** use an itemized, dated inclusion schedule rather than an unspecified presentation.
- **Refund:** define recipient, evidence, procedure, repayment timing and already-delivered items.
- **Employment:** align the supplied-manager clauses with franchisee employment during deployment,
  the agreed costs, SharEat training, six-month cap and return/handover; document the transition.
- **Technology:** reconcile included maintenance, enhancements, outages, continuing support and
  access/export of the franchisee's own records at exit. Do not assume indefinite frozen-version support.
- **Financial reports:** Section 6 requires sales, financial statements and tax reports. The source
  due dates include sales by the 10th and monthly financial statements by the 21st. These are source
  contract terms, not the newly chosen manager month-end cutoff. Head-office visibility still needs
  alignment with the separate-company model in `docs/decisions/ADR-008-multi-company-and-inter-entity-trade.md`.
- **Audit recovery:** an understated sales figure alone does not specify a provable supply debt;
  calculation and evidence are drafting issues, not an approved recovery formula.
- **Default and termination:** reconcile quality cure periods, progressive penalties and immediate
  termination provisions. Complete cross-references, parties, notices, execution and exhibit details.
- **Territory:** verify the site, protected area and treatment of digital sales/other formats;
  the source includes physical-restaurant protection, not an absence of all territorial rights.

## Later rulings must replace superseded draft language

## Legal review references retained from the earlier review

These are issues for counsel to assess, not findings that the entire agreement or every clause is invalid.

- MSME-franchise applicability, disclosures, cooling-off, non-discrimination, notarization and
  registration: [EO 169](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/5/95014).
- Technology-transfer applicability/exemptions and sourcing, improvements, restrictions, liability
  and dispute terms: [IP Code sections 87-92](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1997/ra_8293_1997.html)
  and [IPOPHL preliminary review](https://www.ipophil.gov.ph/technology-transfer-request-for-preliminary-review/).
- Privacy responsibility depends on processing functions, not software ownership alone:
  [NPC definitions](https://privacy.gov.ph/glossary-of-data-privacy-terms/).
- Contract performance and consent: [Civil Code](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/53360).
- Verify contracting identity, signatories, trademarks, landlord consent, restrictions and insurance.
  No registration, legal outcome or insurance availability was independently verified in the review.
- Staff-charge approval/ERP recording was kept separate from payroll deduction. The earlier
  review referenced [Labor Code Implementing Rules, Book III, Rule VIII, sections 13-14](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/85819);
  application to a particular platform loss and the recovery procedure remain separate review work.
