# Future franchise commercial terms

These are the dated terms chosen for future agreements, not amendments to the existing franchise.
The earlier future-agreement Word revision still needs the later responsibility decisions applied.

## Fee and refund

- Initial franchise fee: **PHP 599,000**.
- **80% is refundable with conditions.** The earlier future draft retained the revised source's
  conditions: termination after signing but before opening, with the franchisee not in default.
- At this fee, 80% is **PHP 479,200** and the remaining 20% is **PHP 119,800**.
- No new repayment deadline, extra deduction or treatment of delivered package items was decided.
- Refund administration still needs definition; do not promise an unconditional refund.

The refund percentage is Ian's ruling. The detailed trigger above is the source wording retained
in the earlier draft, not a newly negotiated refund procedure.

## Term and renewal

- The source's **two-year initial term** was retained; Ian changed the renewal, not the initial term.
- Each renewal covers **three years**.
- Renewal payment is **PHP 100,000 once for each three-year renewal**, not annually or midterm.
- A changed renewal fee requires written advice of the amount and reasons **at least 60 days
  before the current franchise term expires**. The changed fee applies to the next renewal.

## Staffing inclusion and uniforms

- SharEat screens, selects and trains **up to seven staff, depending on store needs**.
- Positions to be trained: **cashiers, servers, kitchen staff and dishwashers**.
- Do not fix a headcount for each position or describe seven staff as a guaranteed minimum.
- The provided manager is additional to this staff allocation.
- The franchisee and employees agree compensation and benefits; SharEat does not commit their rates.
- **Uniforms are excluded** from the future package. Current-franchise arrangements are separate.

See [hiring and training](../staff/future-franchise-hiring-and-training.md) for who selects,
employs, trains and pays people. The agreement review identifies other package inclusions that
still need alignment; no new hardware inclusion was decided here.

## Other source provisions to reconcile

The supplied agreements describe supply markup rather than a separate recurring royalty, no
compulsory national advertising fund, initial inventory valued at PHP 100,000, two weeks of staff
training at the Lipa main branch, and tuition waived for up to two management trainees.
These are inherited source provisions to check against the final package schedule, not new
approvals in the responsibility interview or a determination of tax treatment. Attendance and
travel costs, hardware and additional services must follow their specific confirmed terms;
do not infer extra package inclusions from a source summary.
