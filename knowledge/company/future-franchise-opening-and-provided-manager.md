# Future franchised stores — opening, provided manager and handovers

This page describes future franchised stores. Company-owned stores follow their own pages.

## Before opening

1. The franchisee and the provided manager get the store ready. The franchisee assigns the tasks.
2. SharEat must approve the opening in writing.
3. SharEat answers the request to open within 7 days.
4. SharEat approves only when the store meets its opening checklist, and Annex A and Annex B are filled in and signed.

## The first team

- SharEat screens, selects and trains up to 7 staff (cashiers, servers, kitchen staff and dishwashers), depending on the store's size.
- The provided manager is in addition to these 7.
- The franchisee hires each person, agrees their pay and benefits, and handles their papers: contracts, SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG enrolment, and medical requirements.
- Uniforms are not included in the franchise package.

## The SharEat-provided manager

- SharEat provides a store manager for at least 1 month and at most 6 months from opening.
- The franchisee employs the manager on a fixed-term contract. It ends automatically when the manager is released, or at 6 months at the latest. The manager then returns to SharEat.
- Every month, SharEat and the franchisee check whether the store is ready to release the manager.
- Release needs both:
  1. SharEat confirms the store meets its readiness checklist.
  2. The franchisee confirms a ready successor (the franchisee personally, or a manager they hired).
- While deployed, the franchisee pays the manager's salary, meals during work shifts, and accommodation and travel when needed, at the rates on Annex B.
- The manager pays for their own meals outside work.
- SharEat's training of the manager is free.

## Later hires

1. The manager screens and recommends new or replacement staff.
2. The franchisee makes the final choice, agrees pay and benefits, and handles the papers.
3. The manager trains new hires using SharEat's guidelines, checks that their training is complete, and confirms they are ready before they work alone.
4. The ERP training record is (coming). Until then, keep a paper record.

Probation: the manager assesses each new hire and recommends the outcome. The franchisee decides.

## When a manager leaves

The outgoing manager hands over cash, stock, keys, records and open matters to the incoming manager, or to the franchisee if there is no incoming manager. The handover is written down and signed by both.

## ERP and POS access

1. The franchisee asks SharEat for new staff accounts or access changes. SharEat sets them up.
2. When anyone leaves, the franchisee tells SharEat the same day.
3. Only SharEat removes access. SharEat removes it within 72 hours of the franchisee's notice.
4. On the person's last day, the franchisee takes back their store devices, keys and any shared passwords or PINs, so they cannot use the system while removal is pending.
