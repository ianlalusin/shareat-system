# Future franchise brand communications and bookings

Future-franchise policy approved on 4 November 2026. Existing contracts remain separate.
Menu items, current selling prices, hours and active promotions stay in the system.

## Products prices and promotions

- Menu items are centralized. The franchisee submits a new-product request through the ERP/POS
  process; **SharEat approves or rejects it**. Do not invent a duplicate-request requirement.
- For a menu price adjustment, the franchisee raises the need and requests it;
  **SharEat gives final approval** before the change.
- The **franchisee may run local promotions**. Blanket SharEat pre-approval was not adopted.
- SharEat communicates its campaigns and whether participation is required or optional;
  the franchisee joins required campaigns and chooses whether to join optional ones.

## Social-media accounts and public responses

- The franchisee manages the store's social-media accounts within SharEat brand standards.
- Manager and cashier may receive access limited to **posting, messaging and commenting only**.
  Account administration and other functions are not included in this delegation.
- The franchisee immediately removes that access when a manager/cashier leaves.
- **SharEat may moderate when needed.** No blanket SharEat post-preapproval was added.
- The franchisee approves public responses to serious complaints/incidents affecting SharEat's
  reputation, within brand standards and subject to SharEat moderation when needed.
- Limited posting access does not transfer serious-response approval. The manager still
  immediately informs both franchisee and SharEat of serious incidents.

## Reservations group bookings and large events

- **Cashier:** booking details and customer communication for ordinary reservations/group bookings.
- **Manager:** capacity, staffing and service arrangements for those bookings.
- **Franchisee:** large events, including catering and event hosting.

Apply these duties where the services are offered; do not require every franchise to offer all
services. No universal guest-count threshold, event pricing, deposit or cancellation terms were set.
This assignment does not authorize changes to centralized menu prices or add new refund powers.
