# Future franchise responsibilities and scope

These pages record Ian's decisions for **future franchises**, made on 4 November 2026.
They do not amend the existing franchise's agreement or replace company-owned store procedures.
The newer franchise agreement was not yet in use when these decisions were made.

## Role names

- **SharEat** means the head office team and franchisor.
- **Franchisee** means the other business owner operating the franchised store.
- **Store manager** means the person running that store's daily operations.
- A **provided manager** is the manager temporarily supplied by SharEat for opening and handover.
- Avoid the ambiguous role name "franchise owner."

SharEat selects and trains the initial team, provides transitional and continuing support,
approves new products and price changes, administers ERP/POS access and evaluates the store annually.
The franchisee handles employment decisions, payroll, funding, business administration,
financial settlement, store social media and large events, with the approvals detailed below.
The manager supervises daily work, verifies records, follows through on tasks and reports concerns.
Cashiers and other staff perform their assigned work; delegation does not transfer other powers.

## Limits on use

Business responsibility decisions do not themselves change ERP/POS permissions or prove that a
feature is deployed at every store. ERP descriptions in these pages were reported by Ian.
Use the current system for menu items, selling prices, opening hours and active promotions;
these pages assign authority over them rather than copy their changing values.
Contract fees are recorded separately as dated future-agreement terms, not current menu prices.
An unaccepted proposal is not policy. Keep unresolved matters visibly pending.
