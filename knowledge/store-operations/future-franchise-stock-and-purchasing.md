# Future franchise stock purchasing and deliveries

Future-franchise policy approved on 4 November 2026. Existing contracts remain separate.

## Needs orders and receiving

1. Staff monitor their assigned supplies/stock and report needs to the manager.
2. The manager places and follows up routine orders within the franchisee-approved budget.
   Orders beyond budget need franchisee approval.
3. If the regular supplier cannot deliver, the **manager may approve an alternative supplier**.
   Existing purchasing budgets/payment arrangements still apply. No extra SharEat pre-approval
   condition was adopted for this exception; align the future agreement's sourcing clauses.
4. The manager checks that the correct ordered goods arrive and verifies the records.
5. For incorrect/damaged goods, the manager rejects affected items and arranges replacement or
   supplier credit, reporting the issue to the franchisee for payment settlement.

## Payment and receipts

- The franchisee arranges supplier payment.
- At the franchisee's discretion, the manager may pay from cashier cash or the office revolving
  fund under their own agreed limits and terms. Ordering alone does not grant payment authority.
- The cashier keeps supplier receipts. The manager compiles, reports and submits them to the
  franchisee at each month-end and ensures complete/correct ERP records.
- Earlier-due payables must be reported in time for payment, not held until month-end.

## Records and physical counts

- Assigned staff enter stock usage, preparation and transfer records requiring manual ERP entry;
  the manager verifies. Do not duplicate automatically recorded movements.
- Staff count assigned stock; manager verifies results, records discrepancies in ERP and reports.
- **Daily:** display items. **Weekly:** stocks and supplies. **Monthly:** other items defined by franchisee.
- **The franchisee holds stock-adjustment access and handles adjustments/write-offs.** Manager
  verification and discrepancy reporting do not grant adjustment access.
- This future-franchise authority differs from the manager-correction description in the older
  [nightly usage page](daily-usage.md); do not silently apply it to company-owned stores.

## Wastage spoilage damage and loss

Staff enter these records in ERP. The manager verifies and reports routine losses monthly to
the franchisee. Urgent matters such as lost items are reported immediately.
Platform wastage and related staff charges follow the separate
[platform settlement rules](../cash-and-payments/future-franchise-platform-settlement.md).
No universal item classifications, adjustment amounts or automatic staff liability were set.
