# Future franchised stores — who approves what: cash, platforms, customers and store

This page describes future franchised stores. Company-owned stores follow their own pages.

Where a row says franchisee, the named deputy may act instead while the franchisee is away. Peso limits are never written here. They are on the store's Annex A. Features marked (coming) use a paper or spreadsheet record until they are live.

## Cash

| Area | Who does it | Who approves | Limit or rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sales and cash closing | Cashier counts the cash, enters and submits the sales report. Manager checks it and looks into any short or over | Franchisee decides how a difference is settled | No one is charged for a shortage without proof of fault |
| Cash custody | Manager keeps the day's cash in the vault until remittance | None | Only the manager and franchisee hold vault keys. If both are away at closing, the approved backup locks the day's cash in the register inside the locked office |
| Remittance | Manager remits on the agreed schedule (Annex C) | Franchisee or deputy confirms receipt and marks it received in the ERP | Every remittance has a handover record: a slip with amount and date signed or photographed by both, or a bank deposit slip uploaded to the ERP. The ERP record is (coming) |
| Cash advances to staff | Cashier gives an advance only if the ERP allows it | Within the franchisee's rules | Advance limit: Annex A. Advance limits in the ERP are (coming) |
| Expense claims | Staff submit claims | Manager approves staff claims; franchisee approves the manager's | Approval is separate from payment |
| Supplier receipts | Cashier keeps receipts. Manager compiles and submits them at month-end | None | Manager makes sure the ERP records match |
| Revolving fund | Manager pays small store expenses from the fund | Franchisee sets the fund | Fund amount and per-payment limit: Annex A. The ERP fund is (coming) |

## Foodpanda and Grab

| Area | Who does it | Who approves | Limit or rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orders | Manager makes sure orders are prepared and dispatched on time and handles problems with staff and riders | None | None |
| Wastage cases | Refunded wrong or missing items arrive in the ERP. Manager handles each case and appeals to the platform when it is worth it | Manager may propose a staff charge (see the staff charges page) | Staff fault is never assumed |
| Payouts | Franchisee uploads platform payout reports to the ERP and checks them against what was received. File upload is (coming) | Franchisee | Manager explains order-level differences when asked |
| Bank and e-wallet receipts | Franchisee checks receipts against the ERP | Franchisee | Manager explains differences when asked |

## Customers

| Area | Who does it | Who approves | Limit or rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Complaints and refunds | Manager resolves everyday complaints and tells the franchisee about anything involving injury, illness, police or media | Manager up to the refund limit; franchisee above it | Refund limit: Annex A. Per-role refund limits in the POS are (coming) |
| Discounts | Cashier applies existing discounts | None | Cashiers cannot create offers or change prices |
| Complimentary items | Cashier requests | Manager approves | Monthly limit: Annex A. Recorded for the franchisee to see |
| Cancelling an unpaid order | Cashier requests, with a reason | Manager approves | Recorded in the POS |
| Tender correction | Cashier corrects a payment entered with the wrong method and records why | Manager checks afterwards | Refund rules are separate |
| Reservations and groups | Cashier takes the booking. Manager plans capacity and staffing | Franchisee handles large events and catering | None |

## Store and system

| Area | Who does it | Who approves | Limit or rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Equipment and repairs | Manager reports faults and arranges routine maintenance | Manager within budget; franchisee approves major repairs and replacements | Repair limit: Annex A. Emergencies: see the people and stock page |
| Records | Staff and cashiers enter their own records. Manager checks them | None | Financial records are corrected only through the proper process |
| Daily follow-up | Manager checks ERP alerts, open tasks and SharEat memos every day. Memos are (coming) | None | Anything above the manager's authority goes to the franchisee or SharEat |
| Targets | Franchisee sets monthly sales and cost targets with the manager's input. Manager tracks them and recommends fixes in the monthly report. Sales targets in the ERP are (coming) | Franchisee | Targets do not change anyone's spending authority |
| System or internet outage | Manager runs the backup steps, tells the franchisee and SharEat, and checks the manual records once the system is back. Offline POS selling is (coming) | None | Normal limits still apply |
