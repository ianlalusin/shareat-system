# Future franchised stores — who approves what: people, stock and buying

This page describes future franchised stores. Company-owned stores follow their own pages.

Where a row says franchisee, the named deputy may act instead while the franchisee is away. Peso limits are never written here. They are on the store's Annex A. Features marked (coming) use a paper or spreadsheet record until they are live.

## People

| Area | Who does it | Who approves | Limit or rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Schedules | Manager prepares staff shifts and rest days within agreed staffing | Franchisee approves the manager's own schedule | Working-hour rules apply |
| Leave | Manager handles staff leave and keeps enough people on shift | Franchisee approves the manager's own leave | None |
| Overtime | Manager approves staff overtime and records the reason in the ERP | Manager, when the franchisee is away; otherwise as set on Annex A | Overtime limit: Annex A. The store manager has no overtime |
| Coaching and discipline | Manager coaches staff and records warnings, incidents and infractions in the ERP the same day. Infraction records are (coming) | Franchisee decides suspension or dismissal | Employment law and due process apply |
| Payroll | Franchisee prepares, checks and pays payroll from attendance, leave and overtime records | Franchisee only | Manager explains unclear entries when asked |
| Opening and closing | Staff complete their checklists. Manager checks completion. Checklists in the ERP are (coming) | When away, the manager names a trained staff member as opening/closing backup; franchisee approves | The backup gets no vault key and no other manager power |

## Stock and buying

| Area | Who does it | Who approves | Limit or rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orders | Staff report needs. Manager places and follows up routine orders | Manager within budget; franchisee above it | Monthly purchase budget: Annex A. Ordering is not paying |
| Suppliers | Core items (meats, marinades, sauces, anything in a SharEat recipe) come only from the SharEat commissary or a SharEat-approved supplier | Manager may buy other items (vegetables, drinks, supplies) from any supplier when the usual one cannot deliver | Budget and payment rules still apply |
| Deliveries | Manager checks goods against the order, rejects wrong or damaged items, and arranges replacement or credit | Manager | Manager reports the issue to the franchisee for payment |
| Paying suppliers | Franchisee pays | Franchisee | If the franchisee allows, the manager may pay from cashier cash or the revolving fund up to the limit on Annex A |
| Stock records | Staff enter usage, preparation and transfers. Manager checks them | None | Do not re-enter movements the system records by itself |
| Counts and corrections | Staff count. Manager checks the count, records any difference and requests a correction | Franchisee or deputy approves or rejects the correction within 3 days | Only the franchisee or deputy changes stock records or writes stock off |
| Wastage and losses | Staff record wastage, spoilage, damage and loss. Manager checks and reports monthly | None | Lost items are reported to the franchisee at once |
| Emergencies | Manager may spend to prevent spoilage, danger or closure (for example a freezer failure) when the franchisee and deputy cannot be reached | Manager, up to the emergency limit | Emergency limit: Annex A. Keep receipts; tell the franchisee the same day. A loss caused by waiting for approval is not charged to the manager |
