# Opening and closing the store

Every position — cashier, server, kitchen staff, dishwasher, maintenance, and others — has its
own opening checklist and closing checklist, and submits it in the ERP (ERP v1 today). The
checklists are written in plain Tagalog. The items on each checklist are set in the system.

## Opening

1. Each staff member completes and submits their opening checklist: cleaning their workstation
   and setting out the raw materials and items they will use.
2. There is no hard deadline for the opening checklist.
3. Once everything is set up, the day's work starts:
   - the kitchen cooks the side dishes;
   - kitchen staff can accept online orders;
   - servers and the cashier can seat dine-in customers.

## Closing

- Each staff member completes and submits their closing checklist, the same way as opening.
- The closing checklist stays open until **2 a.m. the next day**, so stores that close at
  midnight still have time to finish it.

## Photos and AI checking

- Some checklist items need a photo. The manager decides which items.
- For a photo item, the manager can also turn on AI checking. The AI accepts or rejects the
  photo against the item's passing standard.

## When something fails

- If an item is rejected by the AI, or is not marked complete, the manager is warned. The manager
  makes sure the task is redone, or files it as an infraction against the staff member.
- If a staff member misses their whole checklist, the ERP files an infraction automatically.
