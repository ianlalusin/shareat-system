# Future franchise daily operations and incidents

Future-franchise policy approved on 4 November 2026. Existing contracts remain separate.

## Pre-opening and regular hours

- The franchisee and provided manager coordinate readiness and request SharEat's opening approval.
- The franchisee assigns the tasks. The source agreement requires written SharEat approval for initial opening.
- The franchisee has final approval for changes to regular opening/closing hours. Current hours
  live in the system; no minimum hours or extra head-office approval was newly decided.

## Daily standards and checklists

1. Staff perform food-quality, recipe/portion, storage, sanitation and service tasks.
2. The manager oversees and verifies compliance with SharEat standards daily.
3. The manager assigns opening/closing work and verifies the ERP checklists.
4. If absent, the manager nominates a trained opening/closing backup; the franchisee approves.
   This does not transfer other manager approval powers or vault keys.
5. The manager checks ERP alerts, pending tasks and SharEat memos daily, follows through and
   escalates matters beyond their authority to the franchisee or SharEat as appropriate.
6. Staff/cashiers enter assigned records; the manager ensures completeness and correctness.

The [cash controls](../cash-and-payments/future-franchise-cash-and-sales-controls.md) include
the accepted closing exception when both vault key holders are absent.

## Equipment and service

- The manager monitors equipment, reports faults and arranges routine maintenance within budget.
- The franchisee approves major repairs and replacements; payment follows the agreed delegation.
- The manager resolves routine complaints within SharEat guidelines and escalates serious matters.
- For Grab/Foodpanda orders, the manager oversees timely preparation/dispatch and coordinates
  problems with staff and riders. This does not grant platform account administration.

## Urgent incidents

- Urgent losses, including lost items, must be reported to the franchisee immediately.
- For serious food-safety, injury or reputation incidents, the manager immediately informs
  **both the franchisee and SharEat**.
- For an immediate serious safety risk, the manager may stop affected service or temporarily
  suspend operations without prior approval.
- The **franchisee authorizes resumption**, subject to actual safety and regulatory directions.
  No additional SharEat reopening-approval layer was adopted.
- The franchisee approves serious-incident public responses within brand standards; SharEat
  may moderate when needed. Notification and public-response approval are different duties.

## ERP POS or internet outage

The manager oversees fallback procedures, notifies the franchisee and SharEat, and verifies any
manual records after service returns. This decision does not establish particular offline features,
new financial approval powers or a guaranteed technical response time.
