# Dine-in customer journey

This is the standard journey at every Shareat store. A branch or franchise may add small extras,
such as handing out a voucher when one is running.

## Arrival and ordering

1. The first staff member to see the customer — server, cashier, or anyone — greets them and
   brings them to the cashier.
2. There is always a cashier on duty.
3. The cashier hands over the menu and explains it, then asks which package and which flavors
   the customer wants.
4. The cashier enters the order in the POS, then asks for the customer's name, how many are in
   the group, and any notes, such as allergies or no pork. The kitchen sees all of this, notes
   included (see [allergies and dietary requests](../policies/allergies-and-dietary-requests.md)).
5. The cashier starts the session on the POS. The POS prints a QR code, and the cashier explains
   how to use it: scanning it opens the customer app, where the customer can order on their own
   (see [the customer app](customer-app.md)).
6. The cashier explains the house rules: the [no-leftover policy](../policies/leftover-policy.md)
   and how to order more.
7. A server or the cashier seats the customer at their table and gives them a table number to
   place on the table, so staff know which table they are.

## During the meal

8. Side dishes and drinks are self-service from the buffet table.
9. Refills — mostly meat, rice and cheese — can be ordered two ways:
   - by the customer in the customer app, or
   - by asking a server, who orders on their own device.
10. The kitchen prepares the refill. When it is ready, the kitchen rings a bell (or calls out if
    there is no bell), and a server brings it to the table.

## Paying and leaving

11. The customer goes to the cashier to settle the bill. The bill is given at the cashier, not at
    the table.
12. A server checks the table for leftovers and any extra orders. The cashier confirms extra
    orders with the customer and explains any leftover charge.
13. The customer pays at the cashier using any payment method that store accepts (cash, online
    payment, and others set up per store in the system).
14. For an official receipt, the customer asks the cashier.
15. Paying ends the session automatically. The customer app shows that the session has ended and
    asks for a review: a star rating and a comment.
