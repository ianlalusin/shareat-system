# The customer app during dine-in

The customer opens the app by scanning the QR code the POS prints when their session starts.
While seated, the app offers these buttons:

- **Order refill** — order more of the package items. The kitchen sees the order on its device.
- **Add griller** — ask for an extra griller to cook more or faster. There is a fee; the amount is
  set in the system.
- **Ask for assistance** — the servers' phones and the kitchen tablet show an alert that this
  table number needs help.
- **Loyalty card** — opens the loyalty card page. A customer with a card signs in; a customer
  without one can create one with their phone number.
- **Fun and games** — games the customer can play while dining.
- **À la carte menu** (bottom of the page) — order extra items such as drinks by tapping the
  picture. This appears only when the store's manager has allowed ordering from the app; when
  the manager turns it off, the menu disappears from the app.

When the customer pays at the cashier, the session ends and the app asks for a review: a star
rating and a comment.
