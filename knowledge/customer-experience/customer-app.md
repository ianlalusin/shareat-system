# The customer app during dine-in

The customer opens the app by scanning the QR code the cashier gives them when they are seated.
While seated, the app offers these buttons:

- **Order refill** — order more of the package items. The kitchen prepares it and a server brings
  it to your table.
- **Add griller** — ask for an extra griller to cook more or faster. There is a fee. Ask the
  cashier or a server for the current amount.
- **Ask for assistance** — a staff member is alerted and will come to your table.
- **Loyalty card** — opens the loyalty card page. A customer with a card signs in; a customer
  without one can create one with their phone number.
- **Fun and games** — games the customer can play while dining.
- **À la carte menu** (bottom of the page) — order extra items such as drinks by tapping the
  picture. This is available only at branches that offer ordering from the app.

When the customer pays at the cashier, the session ends and the app asks for a review: a star
rating and a comment.
