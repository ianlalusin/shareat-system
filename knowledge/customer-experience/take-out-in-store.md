# Walk-in take-out

1. The customer orders at the cashier and gives their name. A contact number is optional.
2. The kitchen prepares the order and brings it to the cashier.
3. The cashier hands the order to the customer.

## When to pay

- **Waiting in the store's waiting area:** pay when ordering, or when you get your food.
- **Leaving and coming back later:** pay before you leave.
