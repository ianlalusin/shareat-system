# Foodpanda and Grab payouts

- Customers pay Foodpanda or Grab directly. The store never collects money for these orders.
- **Grab** pays Shareat the next business day — not on Saturdays, Sundays, or holidays.
- **Foodpanda** pays Shareat weekly.
- Foodpanda and Grab orders are not entered in the POS today (see [planned changes](../planned.md)).
- Wrong or missing items refunded by the platform come out of the payout — see [wastage](platform-wastage.md).
