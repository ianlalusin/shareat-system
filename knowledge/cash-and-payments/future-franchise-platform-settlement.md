# Future franchise platform settlement and wastage

Future-franchise responsibility policy approved on 4 November 2026. Existing contracts remain
separate. The ERP feature descriptions below were supplied by Ian, not independently verified
as deployed at every store or in each software version.

## Financial payout settlement

1. The franchisee uploads Grab/Foodpanda invoices using ERP's **settlement feature**.
2. ERP books collection against **accounts receivable**, and commissions/related expenses.
3. The franchisee reconciles actual money received against ERP sales, fees and payout reports.
4. The franchisee consults the manager about order-related discrepancies.

An uploaded invoice alone is not proof of receipt or resolution of a difference. This financial
work differs from the manager's wastage cases. Reconciliation cadence and the owner of follow-up
for a **missing or short payout** have not yet been decided. That follow-up owner is the next question.

## Platform wastage

- Grab/Foodpanda wastage entries are sent automatically to ERP, according to Ian.
- The manager resolves/settles a case **once seen** and submits a platform appeal when needed.
- The manager reviews a relevant case, approves a warranted staff charge when appropriate,
  and records the charge in ERP. Not every platform loss is staff fault.
- If the employee objects, the **franchisee reviews and makes the final internal decision**.
- Approval/ERP recording of a charge does **not** automatically authorize a payroll deduction.
  Recovery/payroll handling remains separate and subject to applicable requirements.
- This approval does not establish staff liability for unrelated cash shortages or every other loss.

## Order fulfillment is separate

The manager oversees timely preparation and dispatch and coordinates issues with staff/riders.
That does not transfer financial reconciliation or grant administration of platform accounts.
The older [payout](platform-payouts.md) and [wastage](platform-wastage.md) pages describe the
previously recorded operational workflows; this page assigns future-franchise duties without
claiming that planned platform integrations are live.
