# Foodpanda and Grab wastage

**Wastage** here means a wrong or missing item on a Foodpanda or Grab order that the platform
refunded to the customer, taken out of Shareat's payout. Today this is the only wastage tracked.

## How it is captured

1. Foodpanda and Grab send notification emails to the company Gmail.
2. A script checks that inbox every day for wastage emails.
3. ERP v1 posts each one to its **wastage** page and assigns it to the store's manager
   automatically.

## What the manager does

1. Fill in which staff member is responsible.
2. Choose how it is handled: raise it with the platform, absorb it as a store expense, or charge
   it to the staff member.
3. Usually it is raised with Foodpanda or Grab, with proof such as photos and CCTV recordings.
   - If the platform pays it back, mark it **resolved**.
   - If the platform does not pay, decide whether it becomes a store expense or is charged to
     the staff member who handled the order.
