# Future franchise cash and sales controls

Future-franchise policy approved on 4 November 2026. Existing contracts remain separate.

## Daily sales cash and remittance

1. The cashier counts/reconciles cash and encodes/submits the sales report in ERP.
2. The manager verifies the report/cash in ERP, investigates shortages/overages and reports them.
3. The **franchisee decides resolution of cash discrepancies**; no automatic employee repayment
   or wage deduction follows from a difference.
4. The manager safeguards the day's money in the vault. **Only manager and franchisee hold vault keys.**
5. If both key holders are absent at closing, the approved closing backup secures the cash
   register in the office **with the money inside**. The backup does not receive a vault key.
6. The manager remits on the day, interval, method and terms agreed with the franchisee.
7. The franchisee verifies actual receipt and marks it received/remitted in ERP. A submitted or
   manager-verified sales report is not acknowledgement that the franchisee received money.

## Customer adjustments

- **Discounts:** cashier may process them. **Complimentary items:** manager approval is required.
  Record both in ERP for franchisee visibility. Processing does not create new offers/prices.
- **Refunds:** manager approves within the franchisee-set limit; franchisee approves above it.
  No standard amount was chosen. Do not apply this approval pattern to discounts/complimentary items.
- **Unpaid order entered by mistake:** cashier requests cancellation, manager approves, and the
  reason is recorded in POS/ERP for franchisee visibility.
- **Wrong payment method:** cashier uses POS **Convert** and records the reason. Manager verifies
  afterward; manager pre-approval is not required. This does not decide paid-sale reversal authority.

## Cash advances

- The **franchisee decides store cash-advance limits and rules** used by ERP.
- The **cashier decides an advance as long as the system permits it**. Do not bypass ERP controls.
- Policy ownership does not independently establish who technically configures a setting.
- This does not transfer payroll work from the franchisee or authorize automatic payroll recovery.

## Expense reimbursements

- The manager approves staff expense reimbursements.
- **The franchisee approves the manager's own reimbursement claims.**
- Payment sources/release arrangements are separate; manager purchase-payment delegation is
  only as agreed with the franchisee, not unlimited release authority.

## Electronic receipts

The franchisee reconciles direct bank-transfer and e-wallet receipts against ERP reports and
consults the manager about discrepancies. Grab/Foodpanda financial settlement and platform
wastage follow [platform settlement](future-franchise-platform-settlement.md).
