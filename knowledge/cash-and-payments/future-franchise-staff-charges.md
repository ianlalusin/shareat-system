# Future franchised stores — charging staff for losses

This page describes future franchised stores. Company-owned stores follow their own pages.

A loss (for example a refunded platform order or a cash shortage) may be charged to a staff member only after all these steps. The same steps apply to the store manager.

1. The manager records the charge in the ERP with the proof (photos, CCTV, records).
2. The employee receives a written notice and may explain in writing.
3. The franchisee decides.
4. Any repayment happens only under a written agreement the employee signs.

A charge in the ERP never means automatic deduction from pay.

Staff fault is never assumed. No one is charged for a cash shortage without proof of fault.

## Law

Wage deductions must follow the Labor Code and its Implementing Rules (Book III, Rule VIII, sections 13 to 14). A lawyer should confirm the procedure before first use.
